# Sound credits

Third-party clips in `Assets/Sounds/`, all from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org).
CC-BY and OGA-BY clips require this attribution; CC0 entries are listed for provenance.
Each clip was cut to mono 22.05 kHz, silence-trimmed and loudness-normalized by
`scripts/build-sfx.sh`. Clips not listed here were made in-house.

"Author" is the OpenGameArt uploader. A few packs re-host earlier work (the Yo Frankie!
set, freesound uploads); their pages name the original creator, and a shipped credits
screen should carry that name too.
