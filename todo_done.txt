G. Hit-state rework: split hitstun / launch / stun (2026-11-05)
Today stun is not its own decision: OnHitRegistered derives hitstun (dv x 0.00293s), stun (dv >= 210, fixed 0.6s), and
hitstop from one number, Tumble is keyed on StunActive, and the crush path inherits the same rule (every billed landing stuns).
- Three flags in CombatState, each with its own source and escape; HitDisadvantageFrames stays the max of them:
  1. Hitstun - control mute + jump/cling block + action entry delay. Authored per move (or dv-derived with floor/cap as now).
     Diminishing on follow-ups by HIT COUNT (see H: full for the 2nd and 3rd consecutive hits, halved from the 4th).
     Applied freely: slashes, throws, crushes, creature hits.
  2. Launch - new Launched flag set by LaunchDecay > 0 hits and by throws. Drives TumbleState (instead of StunActive).
     Ends on landing or tech, never on a timer. Does NOT block jump/cling/ledge (decided; see below) - it exists so
     throws/launchers can be authored without a stun, so Tumble can key on it for the animation, and so the grab and
     crush paths can ask "is this body flying from a hit". Today the physics Launch (Physics/Launch.cs) blocks nothing.
  3. Stun - declared on the hitbox (StunSeconds, default 0), never inferred from dv. Only chosen moves carry it
     (GuardRetaliate, AirHammer sweet spot, wall-slam crush, grab-throw - see below). First-wins while active (no refresh)
     plus a short stun-immunity window after it expires, so a second stunning hit inside a stun can't extend it.
- Crush path passes stun explicitly: wall slam from a launch stuns; plain landings bill HP + hitstun only (fixes "20-tile
  landing = 36f stun"). Pick the crush-stun line and the crush-damage line separately (see the stab wall-slam item above).
- Creature attacks / projectiles lose the accidental impulse >= 525 stun line; re-add per attack where wanted.
- Touch list: Hitbox (+StunSeconds), CombatState.OnHitRegistered, PlayerCharacter crush block (2 call sites), TumbleState
  preconditions, RegisterThrown; retarget TumbleTechTests / HitEvictionTests / stun tests; check the bot's Situation bucket
  (reads "RecoveryAction" as the stunned state).

Grab-throw design goals (think carefully before fixing numbers):
- WANT: throw into a wall, hit them as they bounce off; with skill, chain multiple bounces. The thrown victim must NOT be
  able to spam jump on release to escape the bounce.
- DON'T WANT: throw -> regrab -> throw loops.
- Throw = launch + hitstun + a BRIEF stun (user preference for feel). Since launch does NOT block jump (decided below),
  the hitstun + brief stun are what stop a jump on release; keep the stun short (~12-18f), not 36, and let the wall
  slam's crush-stun be the real punish window.
- Wall slam off a throw/launch = crush-stun (short), which is the bounce-punish window. Re-launching out of that window
  (stab / S3 into the wall again) is what makes multi-bounce possible; stun first-wins + immunity keeps it from being a
  stun chain - the launcher has to actually re-launch each time.
- Anti-regrab (DECIDED): grab can't latch a victim who is Launched, Stunned or in hitstun ("can't grab the helpless").
  One precondition line in GrabAction; makes throw -> regrab structurally impossible. (Alternatives not taken: a regrab
  immunity timer after a throw; per-victim grab staling.)
- Wall tech (DECIDED): buffered jump into the wall just before the slam -> no crush stun, brief i-frames, small pop off
  the wall; mirrors the ground tech in TumbleState (probe the wall the launch is heading into, same slack).
- Launch does NOT block jump (DECIDED): Tumble stays keyed on the stun timer as today; a launched victim who is past
  hitstun/stun may jump mid-air. Revisit only if it turns out to be an escape problem.

H. Big attacks that HIT must be safe (decided 2026-11-05). Measured with ZzzComboTrace.WhipKick_VsMashingVictim: the
whip kick is -15 on hit (victim free at hit+42, attacker at hit+57); by the same arithmetic Stab -17, AirHammer -14,
GroundSlash3 -14, Pulse -33. Only GuardRetaliate is plus on hit. Causes: (1) hitstun caps at 0.70s = 42f while the
heavies' post-hit commitment (rest of Total + EndLag) is 50-60f; (2) hitstop freezes the attacker's action clock but
the victim's HitstunExpireFrame keeps counting through it, so every big hit silently loses ~10f.
- Fix shape: per-move HitstunSecondsOverride on the heavies (bypasses the clamp, as S1/S2 already do).
  PROPOSED NUMBERS (assumes the hitstop fix below lands first; targets ~+2..+5 on hit at point blank, with the launches
  below adding distance on top): Stab 0.85s (51f) | AirWhipKick 0.80s (48f) | AirHammer 0.80s (48f) | GroundSlash3
  0.75s (45f) | Pulse 0.90s (54f; no hitstop, long tail). StunSeconds 0.6 -> 0.8s so Tumble covers the launchers' hitstun.
  PROPOSED LAUNCHES: AirWhipKick LaunchDecay 1500 -> 400 (~191 px/s applied -> ~45px carry) | AirHammer: add a launch,
  decay 500 (outer ~225 px/s -> ~50px; sweet ~900 px/s -> very far, that is the spike) | GroundSlash3: add a launch,
  decay 350 (~169 px/s -> ~40px) | Stab unchanged (decay 100, ~390px) | Pulse and the light slashes unchanged.
- Watch: StunSeconds (0.6s) then ends before hitstun does, so a launched victim drops out of Tumble into Falling with
  muted control for the tail - raise StunSeconds to match or key Tumble on the launch flag (item G).
- Strings are TRUE combos when they land (DECIDED 2026-11-05). From ZzzComboTrace.GroundString_VsMashingVictim: S1->S2
  is true by 3f; S2->S3 is not, because HitstunExtensionScale 0.5 halves S2's 18f to 9 inside S1's window and S3 lands
  10f after the victim is free. Fix: don't halve inside a string - make the scale count hits: full hitstun for the 2nd and
  3rd consecutive hits, 0.5 from the 4th (one counter on CombatState, reset when hitstun lapses). Keeps the anti-lock
  for multi-hit streams (creatures, projectiles, spikes, the wind boss), which is what the halving is really for.
  With full 18f on S2 the victim is free at hit+18 and S3 lands at hit+19 - effectively true; add 1-2f of margin
  (S3 startup 8 -> 7, or S2 override 0.30 -> 0.33s).
- Guard between S2 and S3 is allowed (user OK with it) and already exists: GuardAction enters at <= 12f of hit
  disadvantage, so it can come up ~frames 40-52 after S2 lands; raised at 46+ it is inside the 7f perfect window when S3
  arrives. Ground-only, 60 deg cone, 9f cooldown. The hit-count rule does not change this window.
- Hitstop is cosmetic and must not affect gameplay (decided 2026-11-05). Today it freezes the attacker's action clock
  (PlayerCharacter.Update skips the action's Update/ApplyActionForces) but the victim's HitstunExpireFrame/StunExpireFrame
  are absolute frame numbers that keep counting through the freeze - so every big hit silently costs the attacker the
  hitstop length (~10f). Fix: make it frame-neutral - when ApplyHitstop fires, push the victim's hitstun/stun expiry out by
  the freeze length (or have CombatState.Tick not count down while HitstopActive). Both sides then lose the same frames
  and the exchange is identical with hitstop on or off, just stretched by the freeze. (A render-only freeze would be the
  purist version, but the sim would keep moving bodies under a frozen picture, so keep it sim-side and neutral.)
- Displacement is the other half (DECIDED 2026-11-05: give the heavy hits significantly more knockback, as launches -
  a launch is controllable: Δv sets the start speed, LaunchDecay the carry, ImpactMass the terrain punch, independently).
  Measured pushes today are tiny vs reach (S1 4px, S2 7px, S3 10px, whip kick 12px, against 31-41px slash reach), so a
  victim who recovers first is already in range; Smash gets safety on hit from distance as much as from hitstun.
  Target: heavies carry past slash reach (~40-60px+). Candidates: AirWhipKick LaunchDecay 1500 -> ~400; give AirHammer
  and GroundSlash3 a launch (they have none today - only Stab, the kick and the throw set LaunchDecay); Pulse to taste.
  Light slashes (S1/S2, air slashes) stay as they are. More carry also feeds the wall-slam goal in G.

J. Anti-spam (2026-11-05). Root causes are already queued (F flat damage, H no turn on hit / no displacement, I).
These are the feel levers that add NO end-lag:
- Hit/whiff tail split for the SLASHES (DECIDED): a connect stamps a shorter tail, a whiff the current one. The
  `connected` flag already reaches every OnExitSetFlags. Proposed hit tails ~half: ground slashes 36 -> 18 (S1/S2 only
  matter when the string is dropped - the combo gap already skips the tail on continue; S3 is the real case), air
  slashes 18 -> 10, CrouchSlash 9 -> 6, AirTurn 19 -> 10, DownAir 18.8 -> 10, DashSlash per item I. Heavies (Stab, kick,
  hammer, Pulse) are made safe by their hitstun in H instead; GuardRetaliate is already +18.
- Shorter attack buffer (DECIDED, implement carefully): the swipe/circle gestures are classified by InputParser at the
  release edge, independent of buffer age - the buffer window is only how long a FINISHED intent waits for an action to
  accept it. So: Click/PressEdge lookback for OPENERS 20 -> ~10-12f; keep 20f for the combo steps (EntryFromAnyRecovery
  Index - a click fired 3f into S1 must survive the ~17f until S2 may start, or "three rapid clicks = three slashes"
  breaks); Stab/Circle keep 20 (they are issued at release, the hold itself is unbounded); Jump/Dash keep their own
  windows. Mechanism: pass an explicit maxAgeFrames in the slash preconditions instead of DefaultLookbackFrames. The
  120f MaxAgeFrames stale cap is unrelated and stays.
- Light-hit hitstop (UNDECIDED - user wary of overuse): precedent is universal (Smash hitlag on every hit, ~8f for a
  jab; SF6/GG every hit; Hollow Knight / Hades / Celeste brief hit-pause), the risk is length not presence. Note it was
  tried here already: the 2026-09-01 pass gave jabs 4f, the 2026-09-29 BigHitStrength gate took it away again. If
  retried: 3f, only after hitstop is frame-neutral (H), judge by feel.
- Bot reaction (note): the bots already perceive the OPPONENT 6f stale (BulldogBot.ReactionFrames = 6) but act on their
  OWN actionable frame instantly, which is the frame-perfect retaliation seen in play. If it still reads as mashing
  after H: add a small random first-bet delay (~4-10f) when a behaviour becomes applicable, or raise ReactionFrames.

K. Ground advantages (2026-11-05, candidates - pick). Goal: hopping around must not be optimal. Today the air wins
almost everywhere: MaxAirSpeed 90 vs MaxWalkSpeed 60; air slash tails 18f vs ground 36f; stab dive boost up to 2.5x

 1. Landing lag on aerials - REJECTED (2026-11-05): it conflicts with the movement/action FSM split, where an air attack

    slash is a cheaper S1" gap is closed by the other levers instead: slower air (2), the ground armor (6), the tail
    split in J (an air slash that whiffs still pays its tail), and the damage spread in F (air slashes 0.35/0.5 vs
    ground 0.25/0.30/0.60). If it still pays to hop-slash after those, revisit the air tails (18f vs 36f) directly
    rather than adding a landing state.
 2. Air slower than ground (DECIDED - 1.5x was an oversight). Was 0.7x (42); play-tested to 0.9x (54) with the takeoff
    carrying ground speed (see the play-test block below).
    AirAccel 900 is already half of WalkAccel 1800, so leave accel alone first; "1.0x cap + low accel" is the fallback
    if 0.7x feels dead (with slower falls (L) a low-accel air still reaches the cap given hang time).
 3. Grounded knockback reduction (DECIDED), as Melee crouch-cancel: applies while standing/crouching, not running or
    hopping; reduce the VERTICAL component most so a grounded victim staggers along the floor (stays in reach for the
    wall-slam game in G) instead of popping into the air where air options come back.
    Ground HITSTUN reduction (user suggested - FLAGGED): every true-combo and safe-on-hit number in H was measured on a
    grounded victim, so cutting grounded hitstun un-trues the strings and makes the heavies negative again. If wanted,
    keep it <= ~15% and size H's overrides against the grounded case; otherwise do knockback only.
 4. Grab stays usable in the air (REJECTED ground-only: jump -> grab -> throw into the ground is a wanted sequence; the
    ground crush-stun in F/G is what makes that throw pay off).
 5. Dive stab stays as is (REJECTED the cap: angle-restricted, reads well; revisit if it dominates).
 6. Ground armor (DECIDED - "mostly ground moves get armor"). Proposed: grounded Stab keeps 143, air Stab 0 (the dive
    is its own reward); AirHammer keeps 143 (air-only, 15f startup, unusable without it); AirWhipKick 143 -> 0; Pulse
    grounded 143, air 0; add light armor to grounded GroundSlash3 (~110: tanks S1/S2 at the 100 floor, not CrouchSlash

L. Slower falls so juggling is a threat (DECIDED 2026-11-05: GLOBAL, not hit-only - jump height is unchanged, and a
floaty hopper is easier to hit, so slower falls cost the hopper rather than help them).
- Knobs: Simulation.Gravity (600, read-only by convention; don't touch). QuadraticAirDrag 0.0005 sets terminal speed

### Decisions needed / resolved by the campaign
- RESOLVED (M1): Tumble vs launch. G says Launch drives Tumble but also that launch must not block jump; a movement
  state that persists to landing IS a jump block. Rule used: TumbleState runs while airborne and (StunActive, or
  Launched && HitstunActive). So a launched victim tumbles for the hitstun, regains control when it lapses even if
  still flying, and Launched stays set (for the grab / crush queries) until landing. Launchers (Stab, kick, hammer
  outer, S3) therefore carry NO stun - launch + hitstun only; stun is on GuardRetaliate, AirHammer sweet spot, the throw
  (brief) and the wall-slam crush. H's "StunSeconds 0.6 -> 0.8" is dropped as unnecessary. Override if unwanted.

  PlayerCharacter.OnHit - an Impulse-mode hit from a non-player stuns when it moves the player >= 210 px/s
  (CreatureStunDv), exactly the old line - because removing it outright made the Warlord's cleave combo lethal
